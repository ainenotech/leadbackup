# NenoTechnology — Support FAQs

Edit or replace this file (and add more .md/.txt files in this folder) with
your real company knowledge, then run `python seed_knowledge_base.py` to
load it into the Postgres knowledge base the reply agent retrieves from.
Each blank-line-separated paragraph below becomes its own retrievable chunk,
so keep one topic per paragraph.

## Response times

Our support team replies to customer emails within one business day. For
urgent account access issues, replies typically go out within a few hours
during business hours (Monday–Friday, 9 AM–5 PM IST).

## Getting started / onboarding

After a consultation call is booked, a member of our team sends a short
onboarding checklist by email covering account setup, integration steps,
and a recommended first-week plan. Most customers are fully onboarded
within one to two weeks.

## Pricing

Pricing is quoted per engagement based on scope and is shared directly by
the sales team during or after the consultation call — we don't publish a
fixed price list, since requirements vary a lot between customers.

## Rescheduling a consultation call

Customers can reschedule their booked consultation by replying to the
confirmation email with a few alternative times, and our scheduling agent
will find the next available slot and send an updated confirmation with a
new Microsoft Teams link.

## Cancellation policy

There's no charge or penalty for cancelling a scheduled consultation call.
We just ask for as much notice as possible so the slot can be offered to
another customer.

## Contact

For anything not covered here, customers can simply reply to this email
thread and a team member will follow up personally.
